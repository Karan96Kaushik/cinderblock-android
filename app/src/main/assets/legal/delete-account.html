Delete your Cinderblock account

Bayonet Baron Tech · Cinderblock

You can delete a Cinderblock account and the training data stored for it. Deletion removes the sign-in and the cloud copies of settings, the active plan, training logs, backups, and saved program versions for that account.

In the app

Open Cinderblock, sign in, and go to Settings, Account, Delete account. Confirm the deletion. The app also clears workout logs, runs, body metrics, coach chat, and setup answers stored on that device.

Without the app

Email the developer address on the Google Play listing for Cinderblock from the email address on the account. Use the subject “Delete my Cinderblock account” and include that account email. We delete the account and its cloud training data, or tell you what could not be deleted, within 30 days.
