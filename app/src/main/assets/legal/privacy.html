Privacy Policy

Cinderblock · Bayonet Baron Tech · Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy describes how the Cinderblock Android app handles information. It is a standard template for this app. Bayonet Baron Tech is the developer of Cinderblock.

Information the app handles

On the device, the app stores the training information you enter: workout logs, running sessions, body metrics, plans, setup answers (which can include age and sex if you provide them), settings, and coach chat. It also stores a device identifier used to tell this phone apart from your other devices when you sync.

If you create an account, the app sends your email address and password to the sign-in service, then keeps a session token on the device. The app does not use the password for anything except signing in and resetting it. The app does not show ads and does not collect an advertising identifier.

If you use backup, sync, or the coach, the related logs, plans, metrics, setup answers, and chat text are sent to the services configured for your build of the app. Those services are the ones needed to provide the feature. The app does not sell this information.

How it is used

Information is used to show your training, keep timers and notifications working, remember settings, back up and restore data you ask to sync, and generate coach replies you request. Session tokens are used to confirm that a request belongs to your account.

Where it is stored and who receives it

A copy stays on your phone. Account, backup, and sync data is stored with the cloud service configured for the app (Supabase, in the current service setup). Coach and report requests are sent to the app functions configured for those features. Those providers process the information to perform the service. They are not permitted by this policy to use it for their own advertising.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the service, you, or someone else from harm or fraud.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app posts workout, rest, and running timers you start. Notification permission is optional. Timers on the lock screen need a foreground service while a timer you started is running.

Retention and deletion

Device data stays until you clear it in the app, delete the account, or uninstall. Cloud backups and synced logs stay until you delete them or delete the account.

Signed-in users can delete the account in Settings. That removes the sign-in and asks the cloud service to delete synced settings, the active plan, training logs, backups, and saved program versions for that account. It also clears logs, metrics, coach chat, and setup answers stored on that device.

If you cannot use the app, email the developer address on the Google Play listing from the account email, with the subject “Delete my Cinderblock account.” Include the account email. We will delete the account and its cloud training data, or explain what we could not delete, within 30 days.

Children

Cinderblock is not directed at children under 16. Do not create an account or send personal information if you are under 16.

Security

Sessions and training data are stored for the app’s own use. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Use a unique password and keep the device locked.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the updated text will be available in the app and at the privacy URL on the Play listing.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent to the developer email address published on the Google Play listing for Cinderblock.
